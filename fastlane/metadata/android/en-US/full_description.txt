Buy and sell in Austria — no selling fees, no commission.

Nimmdas is a marketplace for second-hand goods, cars, real estate, jobs and services. A listing goes live in under a minute: type a title, pick your photos, done.


<b>Know straight away whether the price is fair</b>

Every listing shows where the asking price sits — between Super Deal and Above New Price. For electronics and everyday items the current new price is used and adjusted for condition; for cars it is the market value of comparable vehicles. Not a gut feeling, a number.


<b>Finding instead of searching</b>

The map shows every match at once and groups them into clusters as you zoom out. Plus radius search, per-category filters — year, mileage, floor area, rooms — and search agents that tell you as soon as something matching goes online.


<b>List without filling in forms</b>

Type what you are selling. The right category is detected, brand and model are filled in. Whatever is still missing, the app asks for specifically — with suggestions instead of empty fields. Not finished yet? Save as a draft and continue later.


<b>Talk to the seller directly</b>

Chat with images, PDFs, location sharing and price offers that can be accepted or declined with one tap. When typing is too slow, call straight from the chat.


<b>More</b>

• Watchlist for everything you like
• Energy certificate and documents for real estate
• 3D sun path for flats and houses
• Statistics for your own listings
• Earn coins and redeem them for discounts
• German, English and Turkish


<b>About the F-Droid build</b>

This build contains no Google libraries. There are therefore no background notifications — new messages and calls appear while the app is open.

Nimmdas is a client for the nimmdas.at service. An account there is required; the server side is not open source.
